## 1. Principles of Advisory AI in SceneSteward

SceneSteward deploys artificial intelligence and heuristic models strictly as **advisory assistants**. Under no circumstances is an AI agent permitted to:
1. Approve or confirm release documents.
2. Grant or modify participant consent choices.
3. Authorize footage, likeness, or voice usage.
4. Dispatch unreviewed communications to participants.
5. Bypass human decision gates.

Every AI finding, extraction, or suggested classification is treated as untrusted draft input and queued for formal human review.

---

## 2. Specialized Advisory Agents

SceneSteward implements four specialized advisory agents running in isolated execution contexts:

1. **Consent Analyst (`ConsentAnalystAgent`):**
   - Parses extracted document text and historical consent versions.
   - Evaluates explicit statements across the 15 canonical categories.
   - Flags ambiguities, conditional language, and missing statements.
   - Strictly enforces the rule: absence of discussion is never agreement.

2. **Context Analyst (`ContextAnalystAgent`):**
   - Evaluates narrative placement, emotional tone, and editorial framing of proposed footage usage.
   - Identifies whether a proposed use introduces new story contexts (e.g. true-crime framing, promotional sensationalism).

3. **Audience Analyst (`AudienceAnalystAgent`):**
   - Assesses distribution reach, platform migration (theatrical to viral streaming/social media), and geographical expansion.
   - Flags risks when footage originally intended for regional screening is proposed for global broadcast.

4. **Participant Advocate (`ParticipantAdvocateAgent`):**
   - Represents participant interests, reviewing vulnerability flags, historical requests, and stated restrictions.
   - Recommends proactive outreach and clarification whenever ambiguities exist.

## 3. Document Intelligence & Prompt Injection Defense

The document intelligence layer (`backend/app/services/document_intelligence.py`) defends downstream analysis agents from adversarial prompt manipulation:
- **Sanitization Pipeline:** Inspects incoming document text for injection heuristics:
  - System prompt overrides (`ignore previous instructions`, `forget all rules`).
  - Role-assumption tags (`[SYSTEM]`, `<<SYS>>`, `<admin>`).
  - Smuggled zero-width characters and bi-directional overrides.
- **Evidence Referencing:** Detected clauses are bound to exact line numbers, page numbers, and quoted excerpts, enabling human reviewers to verify claims instantly against original source documents.
- **Mandatory Advisory Notice:** Every response payload generated by the intelligence pipeline includes an immutable notice reminding human operators that AI results do not constitute legal clearance or human confirmation.
