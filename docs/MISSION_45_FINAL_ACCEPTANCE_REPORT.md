## 3. Core Documentary Invariants Verified

1. **Silence is Never Consent**: Unchecked categories default to `not_discussed`. An automated pipeline cannot assume consent from participant silence.
2. **Non-Transitive Consent Protection**: Consent for an in-person interview does not permit trailer promotion, third-party AI voice cloning, or synthetic likeness generation.
3. **Mandatory Human Rationale Gate**: AI clearance findings serve exclusively as non-binding advisory inputs. Authorized human staff must submit a documented written rationale to finalize any clearance decision.
4. **Immutable Audit Trail**: All identity, consent, review, and care events are recorded with SHA-256 cryptographic linkage in PostgreSQL.
5. **Dignified Participant Agency**: Participants can inspect all 15 categories via their personal bilingual transparency portal and exercise one-click formal withdrawal at any time.
